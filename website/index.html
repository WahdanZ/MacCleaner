Careful by design

Disk cleaners have earned a bad name with fake scans and scary warnings. Mac Cleaner does the opposite: it shows real sizes, explains every item, and asks before it acts.

  • No alarms, no scare scans. Scanning only reads your disk, and the numbers are what's actually there.
  • Your personal folders are left alone. Documents, Downloads, Desktop, Movies, Pictures, Music and iCloud Drive are never part of junk cleaning.
  • Protect anything. Add folders or files in Settings › Protection, and nothing in the app, its command line or an AI assistant can touch them.
  • Apple's files stay put. System folders and Apple's own apps are never removed.
  • Checked twice. Every safety rule is checked again for each item at the moment it is removed, and a built-in self-test runs on every build.
  • Honest results. It tells you what was actually deleted and what only moved to the Trash, which frees space only when you empty it.

Terminal and AI assistants

Install the mac-cleaner command from Settings › Integrations. Every cleaning command shows the list first and asks you to type yes.

mac-cleaner status
mac-cleaner scan
mac-cleaner clean --recommended
mac-cleaner projects --idle 6

Download and install

  1. Download Mac Cleaner v{{VERSION}}, open the DMG and drag Mac Cleaner into Applications.
  2. Open Mac Cleaner. Because the app isn't notarized by Apple yet, macOS says it can't check it. Click Done, not Move to Trash.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password. You only do this once.

Good to know

On macOS 14 Sonoma you can also Control-click the app and choose Open.

Only download Mac Cleaner from this page or its GitHub releases.

To check the download, compare it with the SHA-256 checksum:

shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/MacCleaner-{{VERSION}}.dmg

With Homebrew:

brew install --cask wahdanz/tap/mac-cleaner

The first launch needs Open Anyway too, until the app is notarized.

Questions

Do I need a disk cleaner at all?

Often you don't. macOS manages a lot on its own. Mac Cleaner is useful when you are short on space, especially with developer tools, simulators and old downloads, and you want to see exactly where the space went before removing anything.

Is it safe?

You see every item before anything happens. Caches and build folders are deleted because apps rebuild them; everything else goes to the Trash. Your personal folders are never part of junk cleaning, and you can protect any folder or file.

Can I undo a cleanup?

Anything that went to the Trash can be put back from the Overview or from Finder until you empty the Trash. Caches and build folders are recreated by their apps and build tools when needed.

Why does it ask for Full Disk Access?

It's optional. Without it, macOS hides the Trash, Mail, Messages and other apps' data, so some sizes come out low. You can turn it on or off any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access.

Why does macOS warn me when I first open it?

The app is signed but not yet notarized by Apple, which needs a paid developer account. Follow the install steps once and macOS remembers your choice.

What data does it collect?

None. There are no accounts and no analytics. Cleanup history and settings stay in your Library folder. Once a day it asks GitHub whether there's a new version; that request carries nothing about you, and you can turn it off in Settings › General.

How do I update it?

Mac Cleaner tells you when a new version is out and shows what changed. Click Install and Relaunch: it downloads the new version, checks it against the release's checksum, and reopens. Or choose Mac Cleaner › Check for Updates….

What can AI assistants do with it?

Scan and report sizes. To clean, they show you the list and wait for your confirmation, under the same rules and protections as the app.

How do I remove Mac Cleaner?

Quit it and drag it from Applications to the Trash. If you installed the Terminal command, run mac-cleaner uninstall-command first.
